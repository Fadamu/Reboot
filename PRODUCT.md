# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individual university students managing and improving their own academic performance. They use REBOOT throughout their academic journey to organize their work, understand setbacks, and recover academically.

## Product Purpose

REBOOT is a serious personal academic operating system. It helps each student organize academic information, track results and GPA/CGPA, manage study work, and turn failed courses into focused recovery journeys. Success means a student can clearly understand their standing, direct their study effort, and clear outstanding courses.

## Positioning

REBOOT connects a student's own courses, academic results, uploaded materials, study activity, topics, quizzes, and recovery performance into an individual academic recovery workflow. It is not a generic school portal.

## Operating Context

Students manage courses and semesters; record and review results using a 4.0 GPA scale; organize notes and course materials; plan and complete study sessions; work with topics and quizzes; and monitor performance. A carryover is a failed course the student is actively trying to clear. Its recovery flow is: results → carryover → understand weakness → study → practice/quizzes → performance → retake → cleared.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- REBOOT uses a 4.0 GPA scale.
- Every user's academic data must remain completely isolated from every other user's data.
- Courses, semesters, results, GPA/CGPA, carryovers, notes/materials, study sessions, topics, quizzes, planner items, missions, and performance belong to the individual authenticated student.
- Carryovers are a core product concept, representing failed courses under active recovery.
- Uploaded course materials and notes are study material for the Study Engine.
- The Study Engine is intended to generate study assistance and quizzes specifically from the student's uploaded materials and studied topics.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is REBOOT.
- The product should feel like a serious personal academic operating system, not a generic school portal.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a Next.js web application with authenticated student accounts; academic profiles; courses, semesters, and results; carryover and recovery screens; notes/material uploads; study sessions and topics; planner, missions, and performance-related surfaces; and quiz-related data and API routes. No external case studies, testimonials, or press evidence are present in the inspected repository context.

## Product Principles

- Keep each student's academic record private, personal, and internally connected.
- Treat academic recovery as an intentional workflow, not merely a status label.
- Make academic standing and next actions understandable at a glance.
- Ground study support and quizzes in the student's own course materials and work.
- Support sustained academic progress across organization, study, practice, and retakes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility, readability, responsive behavior, and a clear information hierarchy are important product requirements.
